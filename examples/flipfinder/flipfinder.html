Import leads (CSV)

Use an export from your county treasurer (tax-delinquent list), code-enforcement records, a probate or lis-pendens list, a driving-for-dollars sheet, or a list tool like PropStream. Columns are matched by name; anything missing is left blank. Recognized columns: address, city, zip, owner, mailing_address, sqft, beds, baths, year_built, arv, assessed_value, mortgage_balance, last_sale_date, tax_delinquent_years, code_violations, pre_foreclosure, probate, vacant, condition (1–5), listed, notes.

Deal assumptions

The area ARV rate is used only when a row has no arv column. Houses built before 1960 add 15% to rehab for systems (electrical, plumbing, roof).

FlipFinder does not fetch records itself: it scores the lists you import. Every figure is an estimate from your inputs; verify ARV with sold comps and rehab with a walkthrough before making an offer. Before contacting owners, scrub numbers against the National Do Not Call Registry, get consent before texting or auto-dialing (TCPA), follow your state's wholesaling and disclosure rules, and treat owners in hardship with respect.
